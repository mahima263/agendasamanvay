IITK Samanvay is IIT Kanpur's flagship Industry-Academia & Innovation Summit, convening leaders from industry, academia, government, startups, PSUs and philanthropy to advance research partnerships and innovation.

Building on the success of its previous editions, the third edition continues to strengthen collaboration through knowledge exchange, technology commercialisation and strategic partnerships that translate cutting-edge research into scalable solutions for national impact.
